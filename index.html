Product rationale · Evidence · Limits

How I got here.

The product focuses on one consequential decision rather than claiming to solve every family-office question at once.

Problem selected

A time-sensitive property decision collides with a ₹40 Cr business funding obligation and a family disagreement. The product helps the advisor structure the meeting, show trade-offs and identify evidence gaps before capital is committed.

Primary user

The primary user is the trusted family advisor, who has received four different perspectives and needs a neutral, evidence-led discussion surface. The family is the secondary user; the family-office lead is the likely model reviewer.

What I left out

Full succession planning, automated property valuation, loan/IRR optimisation and family document storage are out of scope: they need legal/tax expertise, verified property-level data and production-grade privacy controls.

The assumption that changes the answer most

Whether the ₹40 Cr plant funding need is a hard, time-bound cash obligation and what operating buffer is required. Confirm the 24-month cash-flow forecast before treating any financial assets as surplus.

What AI could get wrong — and how it was checked

AI can mistake asking prices for registered transactions, invent rents or redevelopment upside, or call a purchase affordable based on net worth alone. The product labels assumptions, links sources, avoids invented asset valuations and shows liquidity after the funding earmark.
